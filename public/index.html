Our Foundation

Driven by purpose.
Defined by precision.

We exist to push the frontier of autonomous underwater technology — producing elite engineers who solve real-world challenges in ocean exploration and marine infrastructure.

Vision

Lead Malaysia in marine autonomous systems.

Become Southeast Asia's premier university robotics team — advancing AUV technology and setting the standard for deep-tech innovation in higher education.

Mission

Build elite engineers through deep-tech competition.

Empowering UTM students with hands-on mastery in robotics, AI, and systems engineering — through international competition, rigorous research, and collaborative innovation.

Impact

Create technology that works beyond the lab.

Developing systems with real-world applications — underwater inspection, environmental monitoring, and marine exploration — bridging academic research and industry engineering.

How We Recruit

We don't accept open applications. At the start of each academic year we run an open challenge workshop — and from there, standout participants earn a spot on the team.

  • Watch for our annual recruitment workshop announcement
  • Attend and take on the challenge — no prior experience needed
  • Show us your thinking, curiosity, and drive
  • Top performers are invited to join the team
