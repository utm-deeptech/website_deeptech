About us

Engineering for the deep.

We're a multidisciplinary team of students passionate about marine robotics. Our work spans mechanical design, embedded electronics, and software — all coming together to build vehicles that can think and move underwater.

Programming

ROS2, computer vision, autonomy stacks, simulation, and the mission control software that makes the vehicle act on its own.

Electronics

Power systems, microcontrollers, sensor integration, motor drivers, and the wiring that keeps every subsystem alive underwater.

Mechanism

Hull design, waterproofing, thruster mounting, frame fabrication, and CAD work that defines the vehicle's physical shape.
