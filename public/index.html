About us

Engineering for the deep.

We're a multidisciplinary team of students passionate about marine robotics. Our work spans mechanical design, embedded electronics, and software — all coming together to build vehicles that can think and move underwater.

Programming

ROS2, computer vision, autonomy stacks, simulation, and the mission control software that makes the vehicle act on its own.

Electronics

Power systems, microcontrollers, sensor integration, motor drivers, and the wiring that keeps every subsystem alive underwater.

Mechanism

Hull design, waterproofing, thruster mounting, frame fabrication, and CAD work that defines the vehicle's physical shape.

Vision

To position UTM at the national forefront of marine robotics — advancing autonomous underwater vehicle technology and developing innovative engineers who tackle real-world challenges in underwater exploration.

Mission

To empower UTM students through competitive excellence in marine robotics — fostering innovation, building future leaders, and bridging the gap between academic research and real-world engineering.

Target Competition

Singapore AUV
Challenge.

SAUVC is one of Asia's most prestigious autonomous underwater vehicle competitions, held annually in Singapore. Teams from universities across the globe design and build AUVs capable of completing complex missions — fully autonomously, no human control allowed.

Competing at SAUVC is our north star. It's where we test everything we've built — the code, the hardware, the teamwork — against the best student engineers in the region.
